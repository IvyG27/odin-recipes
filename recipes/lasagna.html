Lasagna

Description

This document provides a recipe for lasagna that serves 8 people. It includes instructions for making the sauce with ground beef, onions, peppers and tomatoes. It also provides directions for assembling layers of noodles, ricotta cheese, mozzarella and parmesan in a baking dish.

The lasagna is then baked at 375 degrees Fahrenheit for 45 minutes before serving. The recipe notes that lasagna freezes and reheats well, making it a crowd-pleasing dish for potlucks.

Ingredients

  • 9 to 12 dry or oven-ready pasta sheets
  • 1 pound of ground beef, Italian sausage, or a 50/50 mix of both
  • 1 diced yellow onion and 2 to 3 minced garlic cloves
  • 24 to 32 ounces of marinara sauce, crushed tomatoes, or tomato sauce, plus 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • Italian seasoning or a mix of dried basil, oregano, salt, and black pepper
  • 15 ounces of ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese), 1 large egg, and ¼ cup of fresh chopped parsley
  • 2 to 3 cups of shredded mozzarella cheese and ½ cup of grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Brown the meat: Cook the ground beef, sausage, and diced onion in a large skillet over medium-high heat until the meat is fully browned and the onions are soft. Drain any excess fat.
  2. Simmer the sauce: Stir in the minced garlic and your favorite pasta sauce, then let it simmer on low for 10 to 30 minutes to thicken.
  3. Cook the noodles: Boil the lasagna noodles in a large pot of salted water according to the package instructions until tender yet firm, then drain.
  4. Mix the cheese: Combine the ricotta cheese, egg, half of the mozzarella, and a pinch of salt and pepper in a medium bowl until blended.
  5. Layer the dish: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking pan. Arrange a layer of noodles, spread a portion of the ricotta mixture, and top with more meat sauce and mozzarella. Repeat these layers until you run out of ingredients, finishing with a final layer of noodles, remaining sauce, and the rest of the cheeses.
  6. Bake the lasagna: Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil and bake at 375°F (190°C) for about 35 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 10 to 15 minutes until the top is golden and bubbling. Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
